Plan Notes

the rules, the gate, the fuel

Rules that override the schedule

  1. One variable at a time — volume or long run increase, never both in the same week.
  2. 24-hour rule — pain still present a day later, or altered gait, means repeat the previous week rather than abandon the block.
  3. Cutback weeks are load-bearing — weeks 4, 8, 11 are not optional.
  4. Easy days genuinely easy — HR 135–155, full sentences.
  5. When in doubt, move the session to the bike — same aerobic stimulus, no tissue cost.
  6. Physio exercises beat everything else in this plan.
