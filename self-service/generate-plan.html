Generate your plan with Claude

Stride doesn't generate plans itself — you do that in your own conversation with Claude, using your own account. Nothing here costs Jack anything, and no training details of yours pass through his systems until you choose to upload the result. Three steps:

  1. Fill in the details below and click Build my prompt.
  2. Copy it and paste it into a new conversation at claude.ai (or the Claude app). Talk it through with Claude like you would a coach — ask questions, push back, adjust — until you're happy.
  3. When you're happy, ask Claude for the final JSON (the prompt already tells it to produce this) and bring it back to the Upload plan page.
After Claude gives you the final JSON, copy just that (the code block) and paste it on the Upload plan page — no need to copy the rest of the conversation.
The JSON structure asked for in the prompt is exactly what the Upload plan page checks against. If you edit the prompt before sending it, keep the "OUTPUT FORMAT" section intact or the upload step will reject the result with a list of what doesn't match.
