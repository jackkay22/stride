Upload your plan

Paste in the JSON Claude gave you at the end of your planning conversation, or upload it as a .json file. It's checked against the same format the Generate plan prompt asks Claude for — if something doesn't match, you'll get a specific list of what to fix rather than a generic error.

You already have a plan uploaded. Uploading a new one replaces it completely — any hit/niggle/miss you've logged against sessions that don't reappear in the new file will be lost.
Uploading doesn't touch anyone else's data — your plan is stored against your account only, enforced by the database itself (see the project README if you're curious how).
