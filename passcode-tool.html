Set your Stride passcode

This runs entirely in your browser — the passcode you type below is never sent anywhere, not even to Claude. It only ever leaves this page as a scrambled version of itself.

To finish:
1. On GitHub, open passcode.js in the stride repo
2. Click the pencil (edit) icon
3. Select all the existing text and paste this in its place
4. Click Commit changes
A minute or two later the site updates and your new passcode is live.
Forgot it later? Just come back here, generate a new one, and repeat these steps.
